What it is like to use

Argus plays the media you already have, and adds everything around it.

Everyone gets their own Argus

Choose who is watching before you continue. Each profile keeps its own Continue Watching shelf, AniList/MAL tracker tokens, and playback preferences instead of mixing everyone's history together.

  • Separate watchlists, resume positions, and subtitle styles
  • Lockable tabs and adult content advisory filters per profile
  • Syncs effortlessly across desktop, phone, and projector

What Argus is not.

Argus is a client-side media player. It plays media from sources you connect—such as your own local files, a personal media server, or an addon—and is intended for content you own or are otherwise authorized to access. It does not host, store, or distribute media.

Argus is not affiliated with any third-party media server, addon, catalogue, source, or content provider. Artwork and metadata in the app can come from TMDB, AniList, MyAnimeList, and the addons you install. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
