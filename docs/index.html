What it is like to use

Everything around your media, engineered in Rust.

Argus plays the sources you connect and adds intelligent stream scoring, automated subtitle alignment, and a complete reading and listening studio.

Cinema-Grade Playback

Powered by native libmpv & Whisper AI SubSync

Decodes 4K 10-bit HEVC, AV1, Dolby Vision, AC3, E-AC3, and TrueHD natively without browser codec limits. If a subtitle track is out of sync, built-in Whisper speech recognition and ±0.05s / ±0.25s voice-snap controls lock dialogue to the audio waveform in one click.

Dynamic HUD Subtitle Elevation Cinema Edge Glow Speech-Clarity Audio Mode SVP Motion Interpolation
Binge Without Scrubbing

Playback that gets out of the way

Argus skips intros, recaps, and outros automatically via IntroDB, prefetches the next episode in the background, and probes stream bitrate and speed before you hit play.

What Argus is not.

Argus is a client-side media player and library manager. It plays media from sources you connect—such as your own local files, a personal media server (Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV), or user-installed addons—and is intended strictly for content you own or are otherwise authorized to access. It does not host, store, or distribute any media.

Argus is not affiliated with any third-party media server, addon, catalog, tracker, or content provider. Artwork and metadata in the application may be provided by TMDB, AniList, MyAnimeList, or user-configured extensions. This project uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
