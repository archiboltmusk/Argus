# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Community Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors and community members, regardless of gender, sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity, religion, or technical experience.

## Standards

* Respect diverse perspectives and constructive feedback.
* Focus on community collaboration and civil technical discourse.
* Show empathy toward fellow contributors.
* Treat all participants with professionalism and courtesy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, derogatory language, personal attacks, and disruptive conduct.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable standards and will take appropriate corrective action in response to inappropriate behavior.
